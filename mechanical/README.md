# Mechanical System

This module contains the mechanical design and physical integration documentation for VISIONFORGE.

## Responsibilities

- Conveyor design
- Product fixture and guides
- Camera mounting
- Actuator mounting
- Reject-bin arrangement
- Mechanical alignment
- Component positioning
- Structural stability
- CAD drawings and mechanical documentation

## Hardware

- Mini conveyor
- DC geared motor
- Product fixture
- Camera mount
- Servo/actuator mount
- Reject bin
- Mechanical guides

## Lab Integration

All team members will participate in the physical assembly, wiring, alignment and integration of the complete system in the Mechatronics Lab.

The mechanical design must allow:

- Stable product movement
- Consistent camera positioning
- Repeatable product placement
- Reliable rejection
- Easy maintenance and adjustment
